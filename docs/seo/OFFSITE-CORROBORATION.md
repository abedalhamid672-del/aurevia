# Off-site corroboration policy

Aurevia may maintain a list of official company, creator, product, or editorial profiles only when each URL is verified and legitimately associated.

## Rules

- Never add a placeholder `sameAs` URL.
- Prefer first-party brand and creator profiles with a clear identity match.
- Record URL, owner, verification date, and what identity claim it supports.
- Do not buy links, publish spam, or manufacture reviews and testimonials.
- Community participation must be useful and transparent, not keyword-stuffed promotion.
- Review counts and ratings are shown only when the catalog has a verifiable source.

## Current status

No unverified third-party profiles are emitted in structured data. Add a profile to the citation ledger and verify it before adding it to `sameAs`.
